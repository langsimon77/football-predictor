# Model Changelog

Every change to model structure, features, or code that affects predictions is proposed here first and waits for Lang's approval (spec S0). Parameter and weight updates made automatically by the daily run are not listed here; they are logged by the run.

| Version | Date | Status | Change | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dc_mle_v0` | 24 Sep 2026 | **Live** as challenger (primary until 25 Sep 2026) | Dixon-Coles by weighted maximum likelihood. Decay 0.002 per day, ridge 10, promoted-team prior (attack −0.21, defence +0.14). Locks 1X2 and goals markets. | `reports/backtest_1f.md`: RPS 0.197 on 2023/24 to 2025/26; beats base rates, level with Elo, 0.006 behind the sharp closing market. |
| `elo_v0` | 24 Sep 2026 | **Live** as benchmark | Goal-difference Elo, K 10, home 60, carry 0.75, ordered-logit probabilities. Locks 1X2 only. | Same report: RPS 0.198. |
| `dc_bayes_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live as the primary model** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). | Bayesian hierarchical Dixon-Coles with time decay (0.002 per day), shots-on-target layer, promoted-team priors from second-tier form, NumPyro sampler, diagnostics gate with one retry and fallback to the last good posterior. Locks 1X2, goals markets, and 80% intervals. `dc_mle_v0` and `elo_v0` keep running as challengers. | `reports/backtest_phase2_test.md`: RPS 0.1970 (Elo 0.1982, fast DC 0.1974, market 0.1912); O/U 2.5 log loss 0.6732; 209 of 209 fits passed. Known issue: too timid (calibration slope 1.22). |
| Calibration map for Dixon-Coles probabilities | Phase 3a | **Tested, not applied** (25 Sep 2026). Fixed map: no effect. Monthly map: fixes the slope (1.24 to 1.09) but no clear accuracy gain. Revisit in Phase 4 with stacking. | Beta calibration fitted on tuning-season predictions, applied only if it improves held-out log loss (spec S5.6). | Calibration table in `reports/backtest_phase2_test.md`. |
| `corners_total_poisson_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). | Total corners per match, Poisson, pooled club corner tendency, Elo gap at lock, rolling shots; split into home and away by rolling corner share. Fills the corners columns of the primary row. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_corners_total_test.md`: beats league average (log score −0.009); level with team average. |
| `cards_nb_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). Derby list extended by 10 pairs the same day; the derby effect is re-estimated every run. | Total yellows per match, negative binomial, pooled club discipline, EPL referee effect when known at lock, closeness, rolling fouls, derby, importance. Fills the cards columns of the primary row. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_cards_test.md`: beats league average (−0.019) and team average (−0.017). |
| Per-team corners model (independent home and away counts) | 25 Sep 2026 | Dropped | Summing independent team counts over-spreads the total; home and away corners correlate negatively. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_corners_tune.md`. |
| `challengers_v1` (ordered logit, multinomial, Random Forest, XGBoost) | 26 Sep 2026 | **Live as shadow models** (Lang approved 26 Sep 2026): locked and scored daily, not published. | Home, draw, away from 28 lock-time features: fast Dixon-Coles outputs, Elo gap, rolling shots, corners, fouls, yellows, rest days, league games in the last 14 days, games played, promoted flags, derby, importance. No odds, no news. Settings by time-ordered cross-validation on 2017/18 to 2020/21; monthly refits. | `reports/backtest_phase4.md`: test log loss 0.9746, 0.9739, 0.9752, 0.9775 (Bayesian 0.9759). None clearly beats the Bayesian model. |
| `stack_v1` | 26 Sep 2026 | **Live as a shadow model** (Lang approved 26 Sep 2026): locked and scored daily, not published. Re-test against the Bayesian model at the end of 2026/27. | Linear pool of Elo, fast Dixon-Coles, Bayesian, and the four challengers; weights fitted on 2021/22 and 2022/23 with a 5% floor (multinomial 34%, XGBoost 41%, the rest 5% each). Weekly guarded re-weight designed for Phase 7. | Same report: test log loss 0.9738; minus Bayesian −0.0022 (interval −0.0064 to +0.0019), not clear; minus Elo −0.0049 (clear). Weights flip between near-identical models from one tuning season to the next. |
| Calibration map on the stack | 26 Sep 2026 | **Tested, not applied.** | Method chosen on cross-fitted tuning forecasts: no map (identity) won. Monthly power map: slope 1.11 to 1.05 but log loss +0.0007. | Same report, section 4. |
| `tiers_v1` | 26 Sep 2026 | **Live** on the primary row (Lang approved 26 Sep 2026, promoted-club flag not counted). | High, Medium, Low per market (1X2, over 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 goals, both teams score, corners and yellows lines). Favoured probability sets the start (cut points: top quarter and bottom third of tuning forecasts); 80% interval wider than the tuning 90th percentile, or ensemble against Bayesian disagreement above its 90th percentile, drops one tier; one data flag (source failure, unknown referee for cards, unanswered key question, manager change) caps at Medium, two force Low. Cut points in `data/tiers/thresholds.json`. | Same report, sections 6 and 7: 1X2 hit rates clearly separated (High 74%, Medium 53%, Low 43% for the Bayesian forecast). Over/under separated on 4 of 11 lines. Promoted-club flag picks out forecasts that did better, not worse, than promised, so it is not counted. |
| `news_v1` | 26 Sep 2026 | **Live** (Lang approved 26 Sep 2026, sizes accepted as Assumptions). First questions Wed 7 Oct. | Team news from Lang's Issue ticks scales the Bayesian scoring rates: −2.5% own and +2.5% opponent per regular starter out, −6% more if the main goal threat is out, capped at ±12% (all sizes [A]). A no-news copy (`dc_bayes_v1_nonews`) is locked whenever news moves a forecast. Unanswered questions and manager changes (Wikipedia, confirmed by Lang) become tier flags. | `reports/phase5_dry_run.md`: first real gameweek, all multipliers within the cap, at most 10 questions a day, ranked by expected shift. |
| `stack_v1` weekly re-weight | 26 Sep 2026 | **Live** (approved design, PRD item 10). | Target weights fitted weekly on the backtest pool plus live results, recent matches counting more (half-life one year); weights move by at most 10 points a week, never below 5%, only after 60 scored live matches. Shadow only. | `src/fp/pipeline/weekly.py`; replay of the 17 to 21 Sep week: no move (22 live matches). |
| Steadier stack fit | 26 Sep 2026 | **Tested, not adopted** (Lang approved the test). | Before the weights can first move (about late October), test a small pull towards the current weights in the weekly target fit, in the Phase 4 weekly replay; adopt only if no worse. | `reports/stack_steadiness.md`: accuracy no worse and target jitter 5 times lower, but noise sensitivity not halved (0.009 against 0.006), so the pre-set rule says no. |
