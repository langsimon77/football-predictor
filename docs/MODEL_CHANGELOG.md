# Model Changelog

Every change to model structure, features, or code that affects predictions is proposed here first and waits for Lang's approval (spec S0). Parameter and weight updates made automatically by the daily run are not listed here; they are logged by the run.

| Version | Date | Status | Change | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dc_mle_v0` | 24 Sep 2026 | **Live** as challenger (primary until 25 Sep 2026) | Dixon-Coles by weighted maximum likelihood. Decay 0.002 per day, ridge 10, promoted-team prior (attack −0.21, defence +0.14). Locks 1X2 and goals markets. | `reports/backtest_1f.md`: RPS 0.197 on 2023/24 to 2025/26; beats base rates, level with Elo, 0.006 behind the sharp closing market. |
| `elo_v0` | 24 Sep 2026 | **Live** as benchmark | Goal-difference Elo, K 10, home 60, carry 0.75, ordered-logit probabilities. Locks 1X2 only. | Same report: RPS 0.198. |
| `dc_bayes_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live as the primary model** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). | Bayesian hierarchical Dixon-Coles with time decay (0.002 per day), shots-on-target layer, promoted-team priors from second-tier form, NumPyro sampler, diagnostics gate with one retry and fallback to the last good posterior. Locks 1X2, goals markets, and 80% intervals. `dc_mle_v0` and `elo_v0` keep running as challengers. | `reports/backtest_phase2_test.md`: RPS 0.1970 (Elo 0.1982, fast DC 0.1974, market 0.1912); O/U 2.5 log loss 0.6732; 209 of 209 fits passed. Known issue: too timid (calibration slope 1.22). |
| Calibration map for Dixon-Coles probabilities | Phase 3a | **Tested, not applied** (25 Sep 2026). Fixed map: no effect. Monthly map: fixes the slope (1.24 to 1.09) but no clear accuracy gain. Revisit in Phase 4 with stacking. | Beta calibration fitted on tuning-season predictions, applied only if it improves held-out log loss (spec S5.6). | Calibration table in `reports/backtest_phase2_test.md`. |
| `corners_total_poisson_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). | Total corners per match, Poisson, pooled club corner tendency, Elo gap at lock, rolling shots; split into home and away by rolling corner share. Fills the corners columns of the primary row. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_corners_total_test.md`: beats league average (log score −0.009); level with team average. |
| `cards_nb_v1` | 25 Sep 2026 | **Live** (Lang approved 25 Sep 2026). Derby list extended by 10 pairs the same day; the derby effect is re-estimated every run. | Total yellows per match, negative binomial, pooled club discipline, EPL referee effect when known at lock, closeness, rolling fouls, derby, importance. Fills the cards columns of the primary row. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_cards_test.md`: beats league average (−0.019) and team average (−0.017). |
| Per-team corners model (independent home and away counts) | 25 Sep 2026 | Dropped | Summing independent team counts over-spreads the total; home and away corners correlate negatively. | `reports/backtest_phase3b_corners_tune.md`. |
